# Renoza Product Brief

## One-Liner

Renoza turns South African room photos into realistic renovation plans, local cost ranges, and contractor-ready WhatsApp briefs.

## Target User

Primary:

- South African homeowners and renters planning a room upgrade.
- People who do not know what a fair renovation quote should include.
- Mobile-first users coordinating with contractors over WhatsApp.

Secondary:

- Small contractors who need faster, clearer scopes for quoting jobs.
- Estate agents and landlords preparing properties for rental or resale.

## Main Problem

Renovation is stressful because people struggle to answer:

- What can I realistically do with my budget?
- What should I ask a contractor for?
- Is this quote fair?
- What materials will I need?
- Am I missing hidden costs?

## Product Positioning

Renoza is not just an AI interior design app. It is an AI renovation assistant focused on execution.

Positioning:

> The AI renovation planner for South African homes.

Tagline:

> From room photo to fair quote.

## Differentiation

Renoza should be better than generic AI room tools by being:

- Local: prices in rand, South African suppliers, South African home realities.
- Practical: scopes, budgets, checklists, quote review.
- WhatsApp-native: outputs formatted for how people actually coordinate work.
- Protective: helps users spot vague quotes, risky deposits, missing materials, and unclear labour.
- Budget-constrained: avoids fantasy designs that cannot be done within the selected budget.

## First Killer Feature

Quote Review:

User uploads or pastes a contractor quote. Renoza explains:

- What the quote includes
- What is vague
- What questions to ask
- Whether the price seems low, fair, or high
- What may be missing
- A cleaner rewritten scope
